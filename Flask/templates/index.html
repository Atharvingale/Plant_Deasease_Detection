How the Neural Pipeline Works

Under the hood: multi-stage computer vision trained on 17,500+ lab-verified foliar samples.

Stage 01

Tensor Transformation

Leaf images are dynamically converted to 3-channel RGB tensors, downsampled and normalized to 128×128 dimensions to isolate cellular lesion patterns from background noise.

Stage 02

Residual Feature Extraction

A 34-layer deep ResNet architecture evaluates micro-textures, pustules, necrotic spots, and edge discoloration through residual skip-connections without gradient degradation.

Stage 03

Clinical Treatment Synthesis

Predicted logits are mapped through softmax probability tensors to match the identified pathogen with vetted biological causes and preventative agricultural guidelines.
